## 👋 Welcome

**Abren Technology** is a technology and engineering company specializing in **software development, artificial intelligence, electrical engineering and smart systems**.

We bring software and hardware together to develop practical, innovative solutions for businesses, institutions and individuals. From intelligent digital platforms and AI-powered applications to electrical systems, industrial automation and connected devices, we transform ideas into reliable technology.

## 🤝 About Us

At Abren Technology, we believe technology creates opportunities when people work together. Our name, **Abren**, reflects togetherness and collaboration, and it shapes how we work with our clients, partners and community.

Founded with expertise in both **software development and electrical engineering**, we build technology that is **reliable, scalable, safe and user-focused**: technology that helps businesses grow, improves everyday life and supports Ethiopia's digital and energy transformation.
